A Saturday in two languages.

A rainy Berlin food-market plan becomes a bilingual conversation: mixed English and German, an interruption, confirmed live translation, and a remembered detail.

The user voices are synthesized. Assistant replies come from the real Qwen STT → Ollama Cloud → Kokoro/Piper pipeline. Actual browser playback and interruption cuts are recorded, with original pauses and no narration. This demonstrates behavior, not human microphone accuracy.
