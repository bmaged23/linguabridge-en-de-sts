## Review boundaries

- These are synthetic voices and artificial noise, not a human speech benchmark.
- The browser uses real-time silent WebAudio rendering and native playback completion callbacks. Physical speaker and microphone behavior is not measured.
- Resource samples cover all local worker processes. Whole-GPU figures cannot be attributed exclusively to one model, especially during overlapping stages.
- A phase shorter than the sampling interval may have no resource sample. Sampled peaks are lower bounds.
- The cloud model runs remotely: cloud RAM/VRAM are unavailable; only local resource use and network response latency are measured.
- WER/CER normalize case and punctuation, not number spelling or German compounds. Review flags indicate candidates for inspection, not automatically proven semantic errors.
